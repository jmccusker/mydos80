MyDOS/80 HWLINK Tester 0.1.0-E01 - tester guide
================================================
Copyright (c) 2026 Jim McCusker. Designed and developed by Jim McCusker.

Thank you for testing. This guide says what to expect, what a good
result looks like, and how to send it.

BEFORE
- Your 4P, its two floppy drives, the null-modem cable, the Linux laptop.
- Leave both drives EMPTY at the start. For the floppy part you may use
  one or two disks you do not mind being read (any format; write-protect
  them if you can). Nothing is ever written, but please do not use your
  only copy of anything precious.
- Run `./mydos80-hwtest preflight` and fix what it reports (usually the
  dialout group; it shows the command). Optionally `selftest`.

DURING (about 15-40 minutes)
Follow the screen. You are needed for: two serial-loader resets at the
start (RIGHT SHIFT held while pressing RESET, then ENTER on the PC),
pressing a few keys on the 4P once, inserting disks if you want them
tested, and two short questions. Do not press RESET or BREAK on the 4P
after the second loader reset unless the tester asks.

EXPECTED RESULTS (a healthy 4P)
  Serial loader          PASS ("Found Baud Rate" and "Loading" on the 4P)
  UART profile           PASS (data received, THRE and overrun bits found)
  Stub (diagnostics)     PASS (the dashboard appears on the 4P)
  CPU clock              about 4.0 MHz
  CPU instruction check  PASS (3C1Ch D998h)
  Memory map             PASS; ROM area read only; about 46 1-KB pages RAM
  RAM, Video RAM         PASS, 0 errors
  Keyboard               DONE, with the keys you pressed listed
  Floppy controller      PASS
  Empty drive            "not ready and no index pulse" (or, on some
                         hardware, "READY but no index pulse": ambiguous,
                         also fine to report)
  Drive with a disk      "a medium rotates: about 300 rpm"; stepping PASS
                         (39 steps out to track 0); the survey lists what
                         it found (sides, densities, sectors per track);
                         the surface read lists readable and unreadable
                         sectors. Unreadable sectors on an old disk are a
                         finding about the disk, not a failure of the test.
Anything different from this list is exactly what Jim wants to know.
The report's first line states its evidence: REAL-HARDWARE for your 4P.

AFTER
1. The tester shows "TESTING COMPLETE" and the path of REPORT.txt, in
   ~/mydos80-hwtest-results/<date>-<time>-<name>/.
2. Open REPORT.txt if you like (plain text): check that it contains
   nothing you would not want to send. It holds no ROM content, no disk
   content, and no user or computer name; it does contain the 4P's short
   name and notes you typed, and the serial adapter's make and model.
3. Send REPORT.txt to Jim, by e-mail or whatever way you agreed, with a
   line about anything you noticed (what the 4P's screen showed, noises,
   anything unusual). A photo of the 4P's final screen is welcome.
4. If Jim asks for the detailed records: `mydos80-hwtest bundle DIR` makes
   DIR.tar.gz (it contains the serial transcript; still no ROM or disk
   content).
If the test stopped early, send the report anyway: it says how far it got.

PLEASE ALSO TELL JIM
- Your Linux (for example "Fedora 41" or "Ubuntu 22.04") if the report
  does not show it, and whether the installation was easy.
- Which serial adapter and cable you used.
- Anything confusing in these instructions.
